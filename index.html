Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains how DIVA may handle information when you interact with the DIVA Telegram bot. It must reflect the bot’s actual code, hosting, logging, and AI-provider settings before publication.

1. About DIVA

DIVA (DHRUVV INTELLIGENCE VIRTUAL ASSISTANT) is an AI assistant accessible through Telegram. This policy applies to your interactions with the DIVA bot and related services operated by its owner.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use and how the bot is configured, DIVA may process:

  • Messages, prompts, files, or other content that you choose to send to the bot.
  • Telegram account information made available to bots by Telegram, such as your Telegram user ID, display name, username, language setting, and messages sent to the bot.
  • Basic technical or diagnostic information recorded by the bot’s hosting provider, such as error logs and request timestamps.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information unless a feature explicitly requires it and appropriate safeguards are in place.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to your requests, operate bot commands, maintain conversation context where enabled, troubleshoot errors, protect the service from abuse, and improve reliability. The bot should only retain conversation history or user preferences if those features are enabled in its implementation.

4. AI services and other providers

If DIVA sends your message to an external AI model or another third-party service to fulfil a request, the relevant content may be processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy. Before publishing this policy, identify the providers actually used by your bot and review their data-retention and training settings. Do not claim that data is never shared unless this has been verified.

5. Storage and retention

Information may be stored in the bot’s database, application logs, or third-party systems if the implementation uses them. Retention depends on the bot’s configuration and provider settings. Information should be deleted when it is no longer needed, subject to legitimate security, legal, or operational requirements. Update this section to state the actual storage locations and retention periods used by DIVA.

6. Sharing

DIVA does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the bot, or disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users. List any providers that receive user content before publishing this policy.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep bot tokens, API keys, passwords, and database credentials private, and never publish them in a repository.

8. Your choices and requests

You can stop interacting with DIVA at any time. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of the bot by contacting the operator below. Some information may remain in backups or logs for a limited period where technically necessary or legally required.

9. Children

DIVA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the operator so the issue can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DIVA’s features, providers, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
